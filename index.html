About FCT Vinh Thinh

FCT Vinh Thinh Joint Stock Company is a leading exporter of premium Vietnamese spices and tea. We specialize in providing high-quality cinnamon, star anise, and tea products to international markets.

With years of experience in agricultural export, we ensure consistent quality and reliable supply chain management for our global partners.
