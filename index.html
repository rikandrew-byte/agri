Frequently Asked Export Questions

Key logistics, HS Codes, documentation, and trade terms for international spice & tea buyers.

1. What is your MOQ and monthly supply capacity?

Trial orders start from 500 kg – 1,000 kg. Full 20ft GP and 40ft HC container shipments are supported up to 500 MT/month across cinnamon, star anise, and tea.

2. Which Port of Loading and Incoterms do you offer?

All sea freight departs primarily from Hai Phong Port, Vietnam (VNHPH) under FOB Hai Phong, CFR, or CIF terms via K-LINE, ONE, Evergreen, and major global carriers.

3. What HS Codes apply to your spices and teas?

Cassia Cinnamon & Sliced Twigs: HS 0906.11 / 0906.19 / 0906.20; Lang Son Star Anise: HS 0909.61; Highland Green Tea & Black Tea: HS 0902.10 – 0902.40.

4. What export documents and inspections are included?

Full customs set: Invoice, Packing List, B/L, Certificate of Origin (C/O Form B, D, E, AI, EUR.1, RCEP), Phytosanitary & Fumigation Certificates, plus optional SGS / Vinacontrol inspection.

5. Do you send evaluation samples and what is the lead time?

Yes — representative warehouse samples are dispatched globally via DHL, FedEx, or UPS. Container loading typically takes 10–20 days after order confirmation.

6. What payment methods do you accept?

We support flexible T/T bank transfer and Irrevocable L/C at sight. Contact andrew@fct.vn or WhatsApp/WeChat +84 983 027 776 for a direct FOB/CIF quote within 24 hours.
